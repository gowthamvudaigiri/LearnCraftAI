Odd numbers end in 1, 3, 5, 7, or 9.

Face Value and Place Value

What is the difference?

Face value is the digit itself.

Place value is the value of the digit because of where it sits.

In 352, the digit 5 means 50.

Face value of 5 = 5
Place value of 5 = 50

Odd One Out Patterns 🔍

Find the item that does not follow the same rule as the others. This is a common exam question.

How to solve it

Step 1: Look carefully at every item.

Step 2: Find what three items have in common.

Step 3: Choose the one that follows a different rule.

Example: 2, 4, 7, 8
Three numbers are even. 7 is odd, so 7 is the odd one out.

Exam Tip 🌟

Check these clues:

• Odd or even

• Number of digits

• Increasing or decreasing pattern

• Same ones digit

• Same shape, direction, or size
